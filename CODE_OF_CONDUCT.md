# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

Be respectful, assume good intent while addressing concrete impact, give actionable technical feedback, and protect private or sensitive information. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns through the repository's private maintainer contact or GitHub moderation tools. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical and without retaliation.

This policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant 2.1 and applies in repository spaces and when representing the project publicly.
